Interactive Portfolio Theory

Plot 1: Optimal Choice (Risky Assets Only)

In this world, the curved Efficient Frontier is the "budget line." The investor's optimal portfolio (red dot) is where their personal indifference curve (red dashes) is tangent to this frontier.

Plot 2: Optimal Choice with a Risk-Free Asset (CML)

Here, the straight Capital Market Line (CML) becomes the new, superior "budget line." All investors desire the same Tangency Portfolio (T). Their final choice (red dot) is on the CML, based on their risk aversion.
